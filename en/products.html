Common questions

How do I get a price?

Message us on WhatsApp or call us with the feed type and quantity, and we'll reply with the current price.

Where do I collect my order?

From the mill in Silin, Al Khums, on the coastal road. If you need transport, tell us the location and quantity and we'll work it out with you.

Do you make custom mixes?

Yes. Send us the ratios you need or your vet's formula, and we'll tell you the price and the minimum quantity.

Can I visit the mill?

Yes, during working hours from Saturday to Thursday. Please call us before you come.

Need a price or a specific quantity?

Message us on WhatsApp or give us a call. We reply during working hours.
