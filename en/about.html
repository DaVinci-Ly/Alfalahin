About us

A Libyan feed manufacturer, based in Silin, Al Khums, since 2006.

Who we are

Al Falahin Modern Feed Company was founded on 29 November 2006 in Silin, Al Khums, on the coastal road.

We make feed for poultry, cattle, sheep, goats and camels, and sell it directly to farm owners, breeders and traders. We also make custom mixes to the customer's ratios.

We deal with our customers directly, with no middlemen, and you're welcome to visit the mill during working hours to see the product before you buy.
